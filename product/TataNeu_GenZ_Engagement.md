# Neu Rewards: Giving Gen Z a Reason to Open Tata Neu Every Week

## At a glance

**The challenge:** Tata Neu gives students little reason to return
between purchases. Its home screen can read like a payments app, while
NeuCoins and NeuPass rewards are difficult to understand and do not
create a clear sense of progress.

**The proposal:** Neu Rewards makes value visible and recurring across
the Tata ecosystem, rewarding meaningful actions and giving users a
reason to check progress even when they are not ready to buy.

**Product principle:** Every meaningful action on Neu should add visible
value toward something the user wants.

## Problem

For a Gen Z student, the gap is between purchases: there is no clear
balance to build, progress to check, or weekly reason to open the app.

The teardown identifies three issues:

1.  **The home page does not explain Neu.** The scanner, money transfer
    and recharge experience can make the ecosystem behind Tata Neu feel
    invisible.
2.  **Rewards are hard to understand.** NeuCoins and NeuPass tiers make
    it difficult to understand how small purchases accumulate into
    useful value.
3.  **There is nothing to return to.** Between purchases, users have
    little visible progress or recurring motivation.

The competitive comparison in the source contrasts daily utility in
Blinkit and Zepto, payments in GPay, curiosity in CRED, and social
engagement in Instagram with the limited between-purchase reason to open
Tata Neu.

## Target user and insight

**Primary audience:** Gen Z students who use digital payments and shop
across categories.

The product opportunity is not simply to increase cashback. It is to
make rewards understandable, cumulative and relevant across Tata's
ecosystem.

## Proposed solution: Neu Rewards

Neu Rewards turns scattered rewards into a simple value loop. Users can
see what they earned, where it came from, what it can be used for, and
what they are close to unlocking.

### Core features

-   **Neu Rewards Wallet:** A single dashboard for earned value, its
    source, redemption options and progress toward unlocks.
-   **Instant cashback:** Example proposal: spend ₹500 through Neu UPI
    and receive a ₹50 Neu Benefit, usable across eligible Tata Neu
    purchases.
-   **Weekly rewards:** Complete three meaningful actions in a week to
    unlock a reward.
-   **Referral rewards:** Reward a referral after the friend completes
    their first meaningful action; cap referrals to limit abuse.
-   **Neu Goals:** Choose a category and spend target, then make
    progress through ordinary purchases.
-   **Progress-based rewards:** Unlock capped bonuses after sustained
    participation, such as completing multiple weekly cycles.

The numbers and rewards shown in the presentation are illustrative
proposals, not live Tata Neu offers.

## Example user journey

A student pays a canteen bill using Neu UPI, checks wallet progress the
next day without purchasing, orders groceries through Neu later in the
week, and completes a third meaningful action. The weekly reward
unlocks, creating a reason to return and check progress.

The proposed target is **two to three meaningful Neu visits per week**,
without requiring purchase intent every time. A meaningful engagement
day requires a qualifying action; a passive app open alone does not
count.

## Value proposition

Neu Rewards creates a cross-category value loop: a BigBasket purchase
can contribute toward a future fashion purchase, while a Neu UPI payment
can help unlock a later benefit.

The intended cycle is:

**Act → Earn → Accumulate → Unlock → Spend → Repeat**

## Success metrics

**Primary metric:** Percentage of Gen Z users with at least two
meaningful Neu engagement days per week.

**Supporting metrics:** - Weekly active Gen Z users - Average meaningful
engagement days per week - Weekly reward completion and redemption
rates - Neu UPI adoption and cross-category transactions - Referral
activation and goal completion rates - Four-week Gen Z retention -
Incremental transactions and revenue

**Guardrails:** - Reward cost per incremental user - Cashback and
referral abuse - Reward liability - Purchase cannibalisation - User
complaints and reward confusion

## Rollout and validation

1.  **Validate:** Interview 8--10 Gen Z users and establish baseline
    metrics.
2.  **Pilot:** Run an eight-week test with Gen Z users aged 18--29
    against a control group.
3.  **Scale or stop:** Scale only if meaningful engagement improves and
    reward cost per incremental user remains within the agreed limit.

Start with partner-funded offers and weekly caps. Tie rewards to real
actions, limit referrals, and monitor fraud and reward chasing.

## Key product decisions

-   Make value and progress visible instead of relying on users to
    understand reward tiers.
-   Create reasons to return that do not always require a purchase.
-   Connect rewards across Tata Neu categories.
-   Define meaningful engagement explicitly so app opens do not inflate
    the primary metric.
-   Balance engagement gains against reward cost, fraud and
    cannibalisation.

## Limitations and assumptions

This is a proposed teardown concept, not a shipped product or validated
experiment. The presentation proposes features and pilot metrics; it
does not report measured outcomes. Reward values, eligibility, economics
and user response would need validation.

## Interview talking points

-   Why is the problem about between-purchase engagement rather than
    simply cashback size?
-   How would you distinguish incremental engagement from reward-driven,
    low-value opens?
-   How would you test whether cross-category rewards change behaviour?
-   What guardrails would prevent reward cost, abuse and purchase
    cannibalisation from outweighing retention gains?
-   How would you simplify NeuCoins and NeuPass without creating another
    confusing rewards layer?
